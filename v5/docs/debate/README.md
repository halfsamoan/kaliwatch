# The seven-lens design debate

These files are the record of the debate that produced G3, the "Debate vault" composition (`vault5.js`). Forty-three agents took part. Every round was judged against the user's main reference, the JLC Master Hybris Artistica Calibre 945 Galaxia photo.

| Step | Files | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Proposals | `01-proposal-*.md` | Seven lenses each wrote a full v5 proposal: horologist, astronomer, KALI brand, motion, material, legibility / AOD, and WFF feasibility. |
| 2. Critique | `02-critique-<lens>-<axis>.md` | Each proposal was attacked on three axes: depth, readability and feasibility. |
| 3. Revision | `03-revised-*.md` | Each lens answered its critics in a v2 proposal. |
| 4. Judging | `04-judge-{user,director,engineer}.md` | Three judges ranked the v2s. All three ranked the WFF-feasibility v2 first because it was the only one that matched the photo's two-flange, bright-grisaille anatomy and also passed the v4 schema. They grafted one idea from each runner-up into it: Gyro parallax, Draco in phosphor, the PPI graticule, the sunken sub-disc, the counter-rotating wake and the real planets. |
| 5. Synthesis | `05-final-spec.md` | The winner and its grafts were merged into one build spec. |
| 6. Refutation | `06-refute-{flat,watch,builder}.md` | Three skeptics tried to break 05. The owner's eye argued "it will still look flat and generic", the bench argued burn-in, AOD and Variants, and the builder argued studio feasibility. |
| 7. Final spec | `07-final-spec-v2.md` | Every valid refutation was fixed, and every rejected one was answered. **This is the spec that G3 implements.** |

The debate's one memorable idea: the official Kali dragon arches over Polaris while the real sky, with Draco drawn in phosphor, wheels beneath it once per sidereal day.

G3 deviates from 07 in a few places (round-4 tuning, the sleep timing and the AOD sky clip), and some parts are not built yet. Both lists are in [../REFERENCES.md](../REFERENCES.md) under round 4.
