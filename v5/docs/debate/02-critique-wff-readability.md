# Critique 02 — "Celestial Vault" v5, reviewed from the WFF / readability / AOD lens

Reviewer stance: Wear OS face reviewer who rejects for unreadable time, AOD overruns, burn-in and battery. Harsher reading taken wherever the proposal left room.

**Score: 5 / 10.**

**Verdict:** The architecture is right and, after small syntax corrections, it is buildable in WFF v4 on this watch. As *specified* it will still read flat on the AMOLED, because every depth cue it adds is a shadow cast onto a surface that is too dark to show a shadow. The fix is a change of values (surface luminance, shadow tightness, plate size), not a redesign. Do not approve until FATAL-1 and fixes 1-7 are applied.

## 1. FATAL

### FATAL-1 — The depth stack is built from shadows cast onto black. On an AMOLED, a shadow on black is nothing, so the face is still flat.

What the proposal paints its shadows onto: sky base `#050B0F` (luminance ~3 %), floor `#0B1512` (~5 %), Milky Way `#0E2A33` at 30-60 % (~5-8 %). What it casts: a dragon drop shadow (0,+5, blur 10, 60 %), a plate drop shadow (0,+5, blur 12, 55 %), a lip shadow `#000` 60 %, and a digit shadow (0,+3, blur 6, 65 %). The proposal's own risk #3 says shadows under ~40 % effective opacity collapse the stack. Measured against that:

- **Dragon.** The official logomark is line art: at 148 px wide the whiskers, tail and claws are ~1.8-3.5 px, only the head/neck is a solid ~12-18 px. A Gaussian blur of radius 10 (sigma ~5) on a 2.5 px line has a peak of 2.5/(5 x 2.5) ~ 20 %; times 60 % = 12 % darkening of a 3 % surface = a 0.4 % luminance change. Invisible. Under the solid head it is ~40 % of 3 % = 1.2 %. Invisible. The "raised engraved object" is line art on a star field, and stars of `#C3D3DA` show *through the gaps* in the line art, visually breaking the silhouette. This is the most prominent element on the face and it reads flatter than v4, where the dragon sat on plain black.
- **Hour plate.** An 80 % black ellipse on a 3 % sky is a black slab on black. It is visible only where it happens to cover a star or the Milky Way, plus a 1 px 45 % highlight line. Its 2.5 % wake scale is therefore the scaling of an invisible object. "The plane that visibly floats above the sky" does not exist on screen.
- **Rim lip shadow** (r181-186, 60 % black): on a 3 % sky, a 1.8 % darkening. Invisible.

What survives: the rim bevel (a mid-tone SweepGradient, `#2E4A44` to `#0B1512`, genuinely visible chiaroscuro), the ground edge (an occluder, works regardless of luminance), the gold sun, the rings. At 10:08 on the real panel the Active face is: black, ~90 stars partly washed by teal haze, line-art dragon, digits, three rings, a gold dot. The user will call that v4 with more stars. Galaxia's grisaille works only because its sky is a *mid-tone* fired enamel that white and shadow can both sit on.

**Required change (values, not structure):**
1. Raise the receiving surfaces: sky base `#121E26` (~11 %) inside the aperture, Milky Way bands `#24414C` at 35/50/65 %, baked rim vignette -30 % beyond r170; vault floor `#15221C` with the same radial fall-off to `#000` at r225. Stars then need `#C3D3DA` only for mag <= 1 and `#9FB5C0` otherwise (see fix 2).
2. Make the plate a *dark slab cut into a lighter sky*, which is how the Galaxia's sunken hour ring reads: core `#05080A` at 85 %, a 1.5 px top rim light `#3A5247` at 60 %, a 6 px shadow band below the lower edge (0,+4, blur 4, 70 %). Contrast for the digits goes *up* (paper on `#05080A`), the plate becomes visible, and its 2.5 % wake scale becomes visible.
3. Give the dragon an engraving bed instead of a blur: dilate the official path by 3 px, fill `#000000` at 85 % with a 1 px feather (this masks the stars under the art), then the phosphor art with a *tight* shadow (offsetX 1, offsetY 2, blur 2, 85 %) and the 1 px NW rim light `#D6FFA8`. Rule: anything thinner than 6 px gets blur <= 3 px and opacity >= 75 %, or it has no shadow at all.
4. Keep the rim bevel exactly as proposed; it is the one material cue that already works.

Power cost of (1): AMOLED power scales with luminance; an 11 % grey over ~25 % of the dial in *interactive only* is a ~3 % panel-power increase while the screen is on. AOD is unaffected (all AOD surfaces stay black).

## 2. Required FIXES (approval blocked until done)

1. **Plate geometry.** Replace the rx128 ry50 ellipse by a rounded rectangle whose >= 75 % core covers x113-337, y190-258 (corner radius 34), feathered 14 px outward; PNG 300 x 140 at x75 y158. Digits stay outside the plate group (correct in the proposal). Verify by sampling the plate alpha at (123,197), (123,252), (327,197), (327,252): all must be >= 0.75.
2. **Starlight budget near the time.** `#C3D3DA` with halo only for mag <= 1 (about ten stars in the chart: Sirius, Capella, Vega, Arcturus, Rigel, Procyon, Altair, Betelgeuse, Aldebaran, Pollux). Everything else `#9FB5C0` without halo. No halo may be painted inside r < 150 of the pole (it would touch the plate feather). Reason: paper `#EFF8EC` next to 80 %-luminance dots at the plate edge is a local contrast collapse.
3. **Delete the hand-rolled AOD drift**; the platform shifts the whole frame. Consequently `clock_aod` and `clock_active` share identical coordinates and the "fill in place" signature holds. Keep every AOD-visible pixel >= 10 px from the dial edge: AOD 12 ticks move to r205-213 (Active 60 ticks may stay at r218); AOD sun pointer r203 and hairline r186 are fine. If a drift is ever reinstated, both clocks must live in the same drifted group.
4. **Dayglow cap.** Peak alpha 55 % -> 30 %, ellipse 190 x 150 -> 140 x 110, warm core kept, and it must never reach the plate feather (y >= 158). At 55 % over an 11 % sky the upper vault becomes a ~25 % teal smear for the ten daylight hours in which the user actually looks at the watch, and the deepest plane disappears exactly when it is needed.
5. **Seconds must not vanish.** Either (a) restore v4's 3.3 px phosphor marker on the minute flange at r218, `angle = [SECOND]*6`, no `Animation`, and demote the probe cage to a slow object (no `[SECOND]`), or (b) declare the face second-less and remove the cage. The current design shows seconds only when the probe is above the horizon and clear of the dragon, roughly 8 h/day. Pick one; a seconds display that disappears is a defect, not an instrument.
6. **Date placement.** Baseline y282 puts the glyphs (y271-282) on the plate's feather, over the 10 px extinction band whose top is y279.6 at the centre, with the horizon hairline 7 px below. Either extend the plate core to y290 (the horizon then runs under the plate ends, which is fine and even a depth cue), or move the date onto the ground: date y300, labels y320, values y349, gauges y362, prompt y392 (prompt chord at y392 is x74-376; 172 px of text at x139-311 fits).
7. **Port corrections (verified against the WFF reference, 2026-10-25):**
   - `<Compare expression="ph" value="4">` is invalid: `Compare` takes only `expression`, true/non-zero. Declare eight expressions `ph0..ph7` as `[MOON_PHASE_TYPE] == n`, one `Compare` each.
   - `Arc`/`Ellipse` accept only `Stroke`/`Fill`, `Transform`, `Reference`: no `Variant`. Split "ground" into three `PartDraw`s: ground (always), horizon_active (Variant alpha 0), horizon_aod (alpha 0 -> 255).
   - `TimeText format="hh"` / `"mm"` are not in the documented format list (`hh:mm:ss`, `h:mm`, `hh_10`, `hh_1`, `m`, `mm_10`, `mm_1`, `ss`, ...). If the validator rejects them, use four `TimeText`s (`hh_10`, `hh_1`, `mm_10`, `mm_1`) in 47 px cells: identical glyph positions, since the font is monospace.
   - `[MONTH_S]` / `[DAY_OF_WEEK_S]` are localized. On this user's Korean device the date renders as "25 10월 / 일" in a fallback font, which destroys the terminal look. Build the month and weekday from `[MONTH]` / `[DAY_OF_WEEK]` with `Condition` branches of fixed CDATA, or a bitmap font. The existing on-watch XML already dodges this with `SYNC_TO_DEVICE`; v5 must not regress it.
   - Variant sums: every `duration + startOffset` in the sketch is <= 1.0, which is required ("if the sum is greater than 1.0, startOffset is ignored"). Keep it that way when retuning.
8. **Zodiac names.** 8 px text (~4 sp) is unreadable clutter under the dragon. Replace with twelve 1 x 6 px ticks at r176 (the sun pointer still reads the sign against them), or drop. Keep the 24 RA ticks.
9. **AOD-entry ordering is unverified.** The Variant page documents `startOffset` as "the delay before the start of the transition animation" without saying whether it applies on entry to ambient. If it does, the sky (offset 0) vanishes first and the dragon (offset 0.3) last, the opposite of "planes sinking back". Test on the device; if wrong, set all `startOffset` to 0 and keep the ordering through `duration` only.

Lower priority, must still be tested on the watch before any claim is made: negative `Ellipse` coordinates for the ground (fallback PNG already specified), `SweepGradient` angle origin (undocumented), `blendMode="SCREEN"` and `Font` `Shadow`/`Outline` on Samsung's runtime (community reports of ghosting with text effects on some Wear OS devices; the v4 AOD already depends on `Outline`, so this is a shared risk), and `BooleanConfiguration`-gated reduced-motion duplicates doubling XML size (fine under the 10 MB source limit).

## 3. KEEP (genuinely strong)

- **The time-scale stack and the astronomy are correct**, not decorative. I recomputed: GMST at 2026-01-01 0h UT = 100.6 deg (proposal 100.66); sample LST 2026-10-25 10:08 KST = 177.5 deg (proposal 177.4); chirality (counter-clockwise rotation, east on the right when facing north, RA increasing clockwise in the unrotated chart) is right; the horizon as circle centre (225,71.8) r217.8 passes exactly through the true north point (dec +52.43, 64.6 px below centre) and the east/west points (r154.8), and is within 1.5 px of the true polar-equidistant horizon at azimuth 45 deg; the equinox check (DAY 79 -> lambda 359.9) and the zodiac RA table are right. Mean-sun pointer, moon rule and the stated accuracies (~1 deg, <= 4 deg, +-10 deg) are honest.
- **The ground/horizon as an occluder.** It is the one depth cue in the proposal that works regardless of surface luminance, and it is what makes stars, moon and probe rise and set visibly. Keep it exactly as drawn.
- **AOD discipline.** 1.8-7 % against a 15 % limit under either metric, zero `[SECOND]` in ambient-visible elements, the AOD sky still rotating once per minute, no plate/glow/gauges in AOD. The proposal's lower AOD digit fill `#0C160B` is a sensible choice.
- **The WFF mapping is real, not hand-waved.** Verified against the reference: `Variant` with `duration`/`startOffset` (0-1), `CUBIC_BEZIER` + `controls`, `angleDirection`; `scaleX`/`scaleY`/`angle`/`alpha` transformable on `Group`, `PartImage`, `PartDraw`, so the parallax Variants are legal; `Font` children `Shadow` (`color offsetX offsetY radius`), `Outline`, `OutGlow`, `Upper`, `Template` (Wear OS 4); `Stroke` `dashIntervals`/`dashPhase`/`cap`; `Fill`/`Stroke`/`Font` `color` transformable from v4 with `colorRgb()`, ternary `?:`, `%`, `rad()`, `sin()`; `blendMode` incl. `SCREEN` on `Part*` from Wear OS 5.1; `Arc` `startAngle`/`endAngle` transformable with 0 deg at 12 o'clock; `HOUR_0_23_MINUTE` float, `MOON_PHASE_POSITION` 0-28 days, `MOON_PHASE_TYPE` 0-7, `TIMEZONE_OFFSET_MINUTES_DST` (v3). The Galaxy Watch6 Classic received One UI 8 Watch / Wear OS 6 as a stable release in November 2025, so WFF v4 ambient transitions exist on the target.
- **Memory.** Every ring, tick, gauge and the ground are `PartDraw`; bitmaps total ~2.4 MB at 480 px; ambient ~3.7 MB even if every bitmap is counted. The XML order (static first, sky in the middle, digits and flanges last) follows the memory guide's layer advice.
- **Composition discipline that must not be lost in the fix round:** digit coordinates untouched and outside any scaled group; official dragon path untouched (only rasterisation changes); telemetry and prompt on the black ground, never on stars; gold `#D9A441` as the single warm hue so the eye finds the sun instantly; the 24 px sky band between dragon and plate; the -14 deg flange alignment kept as the wake signature.
